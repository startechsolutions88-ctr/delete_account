# Account Deletion & Data Erasure Request

In compliance with Google Play Data Safety policies, Star Tech Solutions Limited provides this policy regarding the deletion of application accounts and associated data.

## How to Request Account Deletion
To permanently delete your account, please submit a deletion request by emailing our support team at levaskamwajo@gmail.com. Please use the email address associated with your account or include your registered username/phone number so we can verify your identity.

## What Data Will Be Deleted?
Upon verification and processing of your request, the following information will be permanently purged from our databases within 7 business days:
* Your primary account profile credentials (username, phone number, and encrypted password authentication data).
* Active service tokens and session identifiers.
* Internal customer profile preferences linked to your specific account login.

## Data Retention Exception
Please note that any offline physical service records, historical repair diagnostic logs, or financial transaction histories associated with local invoices cannot be deleted immediately and will be retained in accordance with local tax and financial compliance regulations in Zambia.
